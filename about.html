WHY THE DIAMOND? 💎

To me, a diamond represents love, happiness, and lasting commitment. It comes from a family tradition: my dad gave my mom a diamond ring when they married, and years later, my brother-in-law did the same for my sister. That connection made the diamond a small but meaningful symbol throughout my portfolio.
