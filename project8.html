2GETHER

Figma

2gether is a relationship companion app designed to help couples stay connected, remember meaningful moments, and turn the little things in a relationship into shared experiences.

OPEN IN FIGMA ↗

PROJECT OVERVIEW

2gether explores how digital products can support the small moments that help relationships feel connected. Rather than focusing on messaging alone, I designed the app around shared memories, milestones, thoughtful reminders, gifts, and lightweight interactions between partners.

The project allowed me to design a complete mobile experience from onboarding to everyday use while maintaining a consistent visual system across multiple features.

INDIVIDUAL PROJECT SCOPE

  • Concept development
  • UX planning
  • User flow
  • Wireframing
  • Visual design
  • High-fidelity UI
  • Interactive prototyping

WHO IT IS FOR

2gether is designed for couples who want a private digital space for two—whether they want to preserve shared memories, stay connected while apart, or have more time to prepare for meaningful milestones, gifts, and gestures.

THE PROBLEM

STAYING CONNECTED IS MORE THAN JUST STAYING IN TOUCH.

Couples often keep their memories, important dates, conversations, and meaningful moments across different apps. Photo libraries hold memories, calendars hold important dates, and messaging apps hold conversations, while important milestones can still be forgotten in the middle of everyday life.

I wanted to explore what it would feel like if those pieces lived together in one private space designed specifically for two people—and if anniversary reminders arrived early enough to avoid last-minute planning.

THE IDEA

ONE PRIVATE SPACE, BUILT FOR TWO.

2gether brings the little parts of a relationship into one shared experience. From counting days and preserving memories to preparing for anniversaries, discovering thoughtful gifts, sharing quick moments, and sending small reminders of affection, the app is designed to help couples feel present in each other's everyday lives.

VISUAL DIRECTION

WARM, PERSONAL, AND FUNCTIONAL.

The visual direction was designed to feel warm, personal, and affectionate without becoming overly decorative. A soft coral and pink palette creates warmth and intimacy, while rounded cards and generous whitespace keep the interface friendly, approachable, clean, and easy to navigate.

The heart-shaped 2gether identity reinforces the relationship-focused concept and gives the app a recognizable visual element throughout the experience.

01 — GETTING STARTED

A SHARED SPACE STARTS WITH TWO PEOPLE.

The onboarding flow introduces the app, connects partners through an invitation code, and gathers personal information, interests, and love-language preferences to begin shaping the shared experience.

02 — THE RELATIONSHIP HOME

EVERYDAY CONNECTION, IN ONE PLACE.

The dashboard brings total days together, the upcoming anniversary, Memories, Gifts, Widget, Quotes, Recent Moments, and persistent navigation into one clear starting point.

KEY FEATURE

NEVER MISS THE MOMENT.

Instead of waiting until the date arrives, 2gether is designed to notify a partner approximately 1–2 weeks before an important anniversary or milestone. That advance notice creates time to prepare a gift, plan a date, organize a surprise, or make another meaningful gesture.

The purpose is not simply remembering the date—it is having enough time to do something meaningful before it arrives.

03 — THOUGHTFUL GIFTS

A GIFT THAT FEELS PERSONAL.

An upcoming anniversary leads to an advance reminder, which creates time to prepare and explore gift ideas. Suggestions can draw on interests, love language, and shared preferences already provided during onboarding.

Examples in the design include a handwritten letter set, personalized photo album, and coffee tasting kit.

04 — SHARED MEMORIES

KEEPING MEMORIES CLOSE.

Couples can preserve meaningful moments using photos, dates, titles, and short descriptions. I chose a vertical timeline instead of a generic image gallery because it reinforces the feeling of a relationship developing over time.

05 — LIGHTWEIGHT CONNECTION

STAY CLOSE, EVEN APART.

The Widget/Live experience lets partners share a photo, quick note, or small everyday update without needing to begin a full conversation. Showing the in-app flow beside the phone-home-screen concept demonstrates how the experience extends beyond the app itself.

06 — QUOTES

A LITTLE AFFECTION, ANYTIME.

Quotes offer a lightweight way to send affectionate or meaningful messages. The interface supports viewing a quote, liking it, sending it, or moving to another quote.

07 — RELATIONSHIP PROFILE

THE RELATIONSHIP AT A GLANCE.

The profile summarizes the couple identity, connection date, total days together, memory count, love language, shared interests, and relationship connection settings.

DESIGN DECISIONS

01

Duration and upcoming anniversaries are surfaced early enough for meaningful preparation.

02

PERSONALIZED SETUP

Interests and love-language preferences make later features feel more relevant.

03

MEMORIES AS A TIMELINE

Chronological presentation emphasizes a relationship developing over time.

04

LIGHTWEIGHT CONNECTION

Widget/Live moments support quick connection without replacing messaging.

05

CONSISTENT VISUAL SYSTEM

Rounded cards, coral accents, simple icons, large touch targets, and persistent navigation tie the product together.

REFLECTION

Designing 2gether helped me think beyond individual screens and focus more on how features connect across an entire product. Because the app includes onboarding, memories, gifts, widgets, quotes, and profile information, maintaining a consistent experience became just as important as designing each screen.

It also pushed me to think about emotional design differently. Small details such as an anniversary reminder, a shared photo, or a personalized gift suggestion can carry more meaning than adding another complex feature.

If I continued developing the project, the next step would be validating the flows with real users and refining the prototype based on usability feedback.
